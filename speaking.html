Speaking

Topics

What I speak on

Practical, operator-level perspective on the business and technology of streaming — drawn from hands-on experience building and scaling one of the largest AVOD platforms in the U.S.

AVOD & FAST Monetization

How to build sustainable ad-supported monetization systems that generate revenue without degrading the audience experience. The tension between ad load and viewer retention, programmatic strategy, and what it actually takes to run a scaled AVOD business.

CTV Advertising & Platform Strategy

Connected television as an advertising medium and a product platform. CTV-specific ad formats, audience targeting, measurement, attribution, and how streaming product decisions shape ad inventory quality and value.

The Streaming Data Stack

What a mature streaming data infrastructure looks like: instrumentation, pipelines, audience segmentation, real-time and batch analytics, and how to build a data organization that actually informs product and business decisions — not just generates reports.

Data-Driven Decision-Making

How streaming teams use data to make better product, programming, and monetization decisions. Moving from gut-driven to signal-driven decision loops without losing the editorial and creative judgment that makes great streaming products.

Audience Experience in Ad-Supported Streaming

Free streaming is a value exchange: viewers give attention, the platform provides content and monetizes it. How to design that exchange so both sides win — and what breaks down when product and monetization teams optimize independently.

Personalization & Content Discovery at Scale

ML-driven personalization as a practical product layer — not an AI marketing claim. How to build recommendation systems that produce measurable results in viewing time, engagement, and content utilization on a streaming platform.

Streaming Product Strategy

How to think about product strategy for a streaming platform: device coverage, content discovery, monetization integration, audience KPIs, and the unique constraints of building consumer products where the content changes constantly and the competition is always one click away.

Broadcast-to-Streaming Transition

Lessons from helping a broadcast television network build and scale a streaming business — the product, data, and organizational challenges of operating in both worlds simultaneously.

Cross-Functional Product Leadership

How product leaders operate at the intersection of engineering, data, design, editorial, ad sales, and executive stakeholders in a media organization — and what it takes to build alignment across functions with fundamentally different incentives.

Perspective

What I bring to a panel

Most streaming industry conversations happen between people who operate on one side of the business — either the technology side or the content side, either the advertising side or the product side. Zach has worked across all of them.

His perspective is practical and operator-level: what actually happens when you try to implement FAST strategy at scale, what the streaming data stack looks like from the inside, how AVOD monetization decisions get made when product and ad sales want different things, and what it takes to build a personalization system that produces real results rather than just impressive demos.

He speaks from experience building and running these systems — not consulting on them or writing about them.
