Who I Am

Product leader at the intersection of content, data, and monetization

Zach Mannon is a Los Angeles-based product and data executive who has spent more than 15 years building and scaling digital entertainment platforms. His career spans The CW Network, Warner Bros. Television, and Disney Parks & Resorts Online — covering the full entertainment lifecycle from film and television production through streaming distribution, audience growth, and ad monetization.

At The CW, Zach leads product and data strategy across CTV, mobile, and web — scaling a free ad-supported streaming platform to more than 120 million app installs and driving more than $125 million in ad revenue. His work connects product roadmaps, engineering execution, audience analytics, ML-driven personalization, and monetization systems into a unified, measurable operating approach.

He is not a feature-roadmap product manager. He builds platforms, measurement frameworks, and decision systems — and he connects product, engineering, data, design, editorial, ad sales, and marketing around outcomes that matter.
