Who I Am

Product leader at the intersection of content, data, and monetization

I’ve spent more than 15 years building and scaling digital platforms for millions of people, at The CW, Warner Bros. Television, and Disney. I’ve worked across the full arc of the business, from film and TV development and production to streaming apps, audience growth, and ad monetization.

Today I lead product and data for The CW’s streaming platform across connected TV, mobile, and web. I helped scale it to more than 120 million app installs and more than $125 million a year in ad revenue at its peak. My job is connecting product roadmaps, engineering, audience analytics, ML-driven personalization, and monetization into one operating system that the whole company can steer by.
